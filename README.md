# Blue Skies — Conquer Two Massive New Dimensions

Blue Skies 9minecraft Mod adds two massive new worlds to your game. You can travel to the Everbright, a cold and snowy land, or the Everdawn, a warm and darker place. Each world has its own biomes, dirt, wood, plants, and animals. You will find brand new ores and gems underground to craft stronger weapons and tools. The mod also adds huge multiblock dungeons with tough bosses to fight. Build a special portal frame and light it to travel between worlds.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **KingPhygieBoo**, **Lachney**, **SilverDavidMC**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
